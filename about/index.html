The band

Six to Midnight is a five-piece rock band from Central Mass. We play the 90s and 2000s songs you still know every word to.

The set runs from Alice In Chains and Stone Temple Pilots to blink-182, Fall Out Boy and My Chemical Romance, plus one Bon Jovi song we couldn't leave out. We pick songs people want to sing along to, and we play them loud.

It's vocals, two guitars, bass and drums. We bring our own PA and have more than three hours of material, so we can play a full night at a bar or a party without a sound person.
