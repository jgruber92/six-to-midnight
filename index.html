The band

Who we are

Jeff and Kurtis started the band together, and they've been best friends for the last 25 years.

Now we're five guys from Central Mass playing the 90s and 2000s rock you grew up blasting in the car. Grunge, nu metal, pop punk, all of it. If you know every word, that's the point.

We play bars, parties and private events. Give us a stage and a crowd that wants to sing along and we're good.
