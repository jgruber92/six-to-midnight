Six to Midnight

90s & 2000s Rock Central Mass

Bookingsixtomidnight6200@gmail.com@six.to.midnight

Six to Midnight is a five-piece rock band from Central Mass playing 90s and 2000s covers, from Alice In Chains and Foo Fighters to blink-182 and Fall Out Boy. We bring our own PA and more than three hours of songs people know, so you can book us for the whole night.

Quick facts

The short version

  • LineupVocals, two guitars, bass, drums
  • Songs41, plus 3+ hours of material
  • SoundWe bring our own PA
  • AreaCentral Mass

The band is Brandon Ekvall on vocals, Jeff Gruber and Allan Paoletti on guitar, Sam Francis on bass and Kurtis Fotheringham on drums. The set is 41 songs of grunge, alt rock, pop punk and nu metal, the stuff that was all over rock radio in the 90s and 2000s. We play bars, parties and private events.
